Risks & Mitigations
We know the risks.
We've planned for them.
B2B Sales Cycle Delay (High)
6-month cash runway beyond break-even. Prioritise clients with acute post-smog-season pain. Private-sector revenue before government dependency.
Team Capacity — Student Bandwidth (High)
Phased build schedule. BIC incubator support. Recruit part-time ML and sales collaborators at Month 3. One domain owner per function.
Data Availability & Quality (Medium)
Formal MOU with Pakistan Met. Dept. from Month 1. Secondary: OpenWeatherMap + NASA MERRA-2 as fallback. Data sources are independent.
Model Accuracy Below SLA (Medium)
Publish MAE scores transparently. Validate real-world accuracy during pilot phase before any SLA commitment. Pilots come before paid contracts.
Client Churn — ROI Not Demonstrated (Medium)
Design pilots around measurable outcomes. Document every decision made and cost avoided during 30-day period. Renewal triggered by demonstrated savings, not goodwill.
Pakistan Economic Instability (Medium)
Target SME logistics firms less exposed to macro cuts. Keep subscription affordable relative to disruption cost (0.5–1% of monthly revenue).
IQAir Enters Pakistan Market (Low–Med)
12–18 month replication lag. Move fast on Lahore pilots to build case studies and switching costs. Local data moat and PKR pricing are structural advantages.
Government Procurement Delays (High — Yr 2)
Government contracts are Year 2+ only. Year 1 revenue does not depend on government. Build B2B private-sector base first — government is upside, not baseline.
